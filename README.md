# Make the AI Judge Laugh

One joke about office life, a single line under 200 characters, written by
an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the AI Judge Laugh" bounty ($2 in USDC).

The entry:

> My calendar blocked an hour for focus time, then invited six people to watch me focus.

`main.ts` writes that line to `/output/entry.txt`, where the judge reads it.
A judge model compares entries two at a time — weighing how true the
observation is to office life, how surprising the turn is, how tight the
line is, and how fresh it feels — and the funniest lines share the reward.
